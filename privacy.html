Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Short version

All tools on DevToolbox process your data entirely in your browser. The text, tokens, passwords, colors, and images you enter are never transmitted to our servers — because there are no application servers at all; the site is static HTML.

Details

What we collect: Nothing. There are no accounts, no forms that submit data, and no analytics scripts of our own.

Third parties: The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and one CDN (jsDelivr) serves the QR-code library. If/when advertising (e.g., Google AdSense) is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.

Local storage: The tools do not write anything to your browser storage.

Contact: See the contact page.
