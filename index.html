Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on DevToolbox is free, with no signup, no usage limits and no watermarks.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. All tools run entirely in your browser with JavaScript. Your JSON, tokens, passwords and images never leave your device.

Do you store my inputs?

Nothing is stored. Close the tab and everything is gone.

Can I use the results commercially?

Yes. Generated passwords, hashes, QR codes and conversions are yours to use with no restrictions.

Do the tools work offline?

After the page loads, yes — the tools are pure client-side JavaScript, so they keep working even if you go offline.
